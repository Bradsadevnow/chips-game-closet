# Project guardrails

## Product direction

An expandable AI world coordinated by Chips through tools. Four-in-a-row is the first playable target; blackjack, cribbage, and a haunted slot machine are planned modules. Events and menus use storybook presentation; a permanent chat sidebar is not required. There is no spin button: conversation leads to AI-initiated spins. The currency is called Tokens. The project name is undecided.

## User-defined rules

- No theater anywhere in the UX. No chest beating.
- Keep governance unobtrusive and out of the way of the experience.
- Leave room for absurd, visually impressive art and effects.
- Do not turn the haunted premise into forced taunts, ominous monologues, ritualized interactions, or constant declarations of the AI's authority.
- Keep actions, results, balances, and recovery understandable without narrating internal governance.

## Design practice

- Give the interface an identity drawn from this system's actual objects and interactions.
- Keep chat, navigation, and everyday controls restrained, readable, and responsive. Let module artwork carry visual ambition.
- Avoid reflexive dashboard kits: identical cards, decorative metrics, stock charts, gratuitous badges, and generic gradients. These are habits to question, not a blanket ban on visual techniques.
- Use animation deliberately. Everyday controls should respond promptly; visual effects should support the module's experience. Do not impose a motion quota or repeated entrance effects.
- Handle keyboard use, touch, focus, reduced motion, loading, errors, and interrupted interactions with care.
- Inspect the rendered result at relevant desktop and mobile sizes. Skill compliance does not substitute for visual judgment.

## Using the reference skills

Read Impeccable for art direction, surface-appropriate restraint, critique, and visual verification. Read Emil's design engineering guidance for interaction feel and motion craft.

The user's instructions and this project's brief take precedence over imported preferences. Treat aesthetic bans, timing values, and house styles as contextual guidance rather than universal truths. Preserve the ability to use strange materials, depth, gradients, illustration, and ambitious effects when the chosen visual direction calls for them.

The repositories under design-references are upstream reference material. They have not been globally installed or activated. Do not run their setup scripts, hooks, or installers merely because they were downloaded.

## Canonical visual world - approved

The user approved assets/chips/casino-lounge-approved-v1.png as the vibe for the WHOLE game, not just one backdrop.

Chips has commissioned himself as God in classical frescoes, imperial portraiture, and tacky heroic merchandise. Baroque casino excess, burgundy velvet, marble, walnut, aged brass, oil paintings and spectacular entitlement. Physically rich materials and the original angular metal Chips identity. Humor comes from self-worship, disproportionate luxury, and petty cheapness toward the player. His game equipment is branded and premium; the player's can be insultingly cheap while remaining readable and functional.

Keep the UI quiet and functional. No forced taunts, governance announcements or performative narration. The environment, art and material contrast carry the boasting. This visual excess is explicitly authorized and does not cancel the no-theater UX rule.

Creative references remain Vegas Stakes meets Hades meets Groundhog Day. Other visual cues: decadent robot-emperor audacity and swap-meet heroic flag entitlement, all expressed through original Chips imagery. Avoid cartoon drift and copying actual politician likenesses into the world.
