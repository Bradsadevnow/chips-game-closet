# Playing cards: concept direction v1

The same Chips appears across the deck. His face stays consistent; his costumes, props, and increasingly ridiculous self-image supply the jokes. Ordinary cards retain conventional ranks and suits with clear corner indices.

Visual direction: engraved casino cards on ivory stock, burgundy and near-black ink, restrained gold. Absurd detailed portraits inside a coherent, playable card frame.

First concept sheet:
- King of Diamonds: enormous royal robes, cheap plastic patio throne, toilet-plunger scepter.
- Queen of Hearts: Chips in a pageant wig, sash reading MISS APPROPRIATION, bouquet of overdue invoices.
- Jack of Clubs: fake muscle suit, tiny real robot hands, sack of Tokens.
- Ace of Spades: nested portraits of Chips inside a self-aggrandizing crest.
- AUDIT wild card: accountant visor over crown, eating a receipt, concealing scissors. NOTHING TO SEE HERE.
- Card back: two-way symmetric ornament made of his portrait, crowns, hands, and Tokens.

These are artwork concepts. Wildcard effects are not yet specified. Review the sheet before producing a full deck. Production cards will need separate assets, verified indices, and an actually symmetric back.
